Step 5 — Sliders

This step wires up the sliders that control burner, air, and drum.

  1. In Artisan, open Config → Events → Sliders tab.
  2. For each slider below, enter its WebSocket Command, min/max, and step exactly as shown.
SliderWebSocket CommandMin–MaxStep
Airsend({{ "pushMessage": "DRAW {}" }})0 – 1005
Drumsend({{ "pushMessage": "DRUM {}" }})0 – 1005
Burnersend({{ "pushMessage": "HEAT {}" }})0 – 1005
Step must be set to 5. Setting it to 1 means even a small slider movement sends far more commands, much faster, which can make the connection to the roaster unstable.
