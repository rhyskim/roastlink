Setup Guide

Follow these steps in Artisan, in order, to connect it to RoastLink. The order matters, so please go through them one at a time.

  1. Step 1 — Device

    Tell Artisan where to read temperature from.

  2. Step 2 — Port

    Connect Artisan to RoastLink's address.

  3. Step 3 — Sampling

    Decide how often to check the temperature.

  4. Step 4 — Event Buttons

    Wire up the preheat/charge/drop buttons.

  5. Step 5 — Sliders

    Wire up the burner/air/drum control sliders.

  6. Step 6 — Disable Quantifiers Required

    Skip this and your sliders will move on their own.

  7. Roasting Walkthrough

    The actual sequence from preheat to drop.

Once everything above is set up, move on to the Roasting Walkthrough.
